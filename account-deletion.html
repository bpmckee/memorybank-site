Delete your Memory Bank account

Last updated: September 25, 2026

You can request account deletion without installing or reopening the app.

In the app

Open You > Account > Delete Account, type the requested confirmation, and confirm. You may need to sign in again. If you use Sign in with Apple, use that method so we can revoke its authorization.

By email

Email bpmckee3@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Memory Bank account. Send from the email associated with your account when possible, or provide your public profile ID. We verify account ownership before proceeding and generally process verified email requests within 30 days. We will never ask for your password, verification code, or payment to delete an account.

Delete specific data while keeping your account

You can delete individual stories, moments, and uploaded media from their menus in the app. For help deleting specific data without closing your account, email bpmckee3@gmail.com and describe which items you want removed. Do not include passwords, verification codes, or private invitation links. We verify ownership before processing a request; the retention limits below also apply.

What is deleted

Deletion disables your account and removes owned stories, draft and published versions, uploaded media, profile, relationships, invitations, comments, and other associated app data from active systems. Account-associated contributions in shared stories and stored in-app copies are removed or redacted. Affected history and customizations may also be cleared. Other authors' material can remain. Screenshots and exports outside Memory Bank are outside our control.

Timing and retained information

Cleanup runs in batches and retries interruptions. We send confirmation when active-system cleanup finishes. Apple authorization revocation can take longer during provider outages; its retry token is deleted when revocation succeeds. Provider backups and service logs may persist for up to 90 days through routine rotation, and narrowly required legal or security records may be retained for that purpose. Contact the address above if you have not received confirmation or need help with remaining data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
