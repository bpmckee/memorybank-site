Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Memory Bank is a journaling app created by William McKee. This Privacy Policy explains what information Memory Bank collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at bpmckee3@gmail.com.

Information You Provide

Memory Bank stores the content you choose to create or upload, including journals, entries, moments, text fields, captions, locations, visits, photos, videos, and related metadata. This content is used to provide the journaling, timeline, map, media, and Smart Suggestions features in the app.

If you create an account or sign in, we may store account information such as your email address, user identifier, authentication provider information, and session information so you can access your account.

Location Information

Memory Bank can use location in several ways:

  • Manual location features: When you tag a moment, add a place, view nearby places, or use the map, Memory Bank may use your location while the app is open.
  • Smart Suggestions while using the app: If enabled, Memory Bank may use your location while the app is open to suggest moments based on places near you.
  • Background Smart Suggestions: If you choose the Background option, Memory Bank may use your location in the background, including when the app is closed, to detect places you visit and suggest moments to log.

You can control location suggestions in the app under You > Smart Suggestions. The available choices are Background, While Using, and Off. You can also change or revoke location permissions in your device settings.

Memory Bank does not sell your location data and does not use location data for advertising.

Photo Library, Camera, and Media

If you grant access, Memory Bank can use your photo library and camera so you can add photos or videos to moments and entries. If Photo Smart Suggestions are enabled, Memory Bank may scan your photo library while the app is open for photos or videos with location metadata and suggest moments you can log.

You can control photo suggestions in the app under You > Smart Suggestions. You can also change or revoke photo permissions in your device settings.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

If you use voice capture or transcription features, Memory Bank may request microphone and speech recognition permissions. Audio may be processed by the operating system or speech recognition provider to convert your voice into text. The resulting text may be saved in your Memory Bank content if you choose to keep it.

Place Search and Maps

Memory Bank uses map and place services to show maps, search for places, reverse geocode coordinates, and display place names. Depending on platform and configuration, this may involve Apple Maps, Google Maps, Radar, Geoapify, or similar place search services. Place queries and approximate coordinates may be sent to these services when needed to provide place search or place-name lookup. Memory Bank may cache provider responses to improve performance and reduce repeated provider calls.

Storage and Service Providers

Memory Bank uses service providers to run the app, authenticate users, store app data, store uploaded media, send email, and provide maps, place search, and speech-related functionality. These providers may process information only as needed to provide the service.

Service providers include Expo, Convex, Better Auth, Cloudflare R2 storage, Apple sign-in, Google sign-in, Google Play Services, Apple Maps, Google Maps, Radar, Geoapify, Resend, Sentry, and OpenAI for shared-content safety checks. Which location and speech providers receive a request depends on the platform and the feature you use.

Diagnostics and Automatically Collected Information

The app and its service providers may collect technical information needed to operate, secure, debug, and improve the service, such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, error logs, and basic usage events. Release builds use Sentry when crash reporting is configured. Error reports may include stack traces, device and operating-system details, app version, and a short sequence of diagnostic events leading to a failure. These events describe screen types, operation names, connection state, error codes, timings, and media sizes or counts. We filter report data to exclude journal text, photos, precise locations, account identifiers, authentication credentials, and signed media URLs. We do not enable session replay, screenshots, advertising tracking, performance tracing, profiling, or a separate usage-analytics stream. Reports may still contain incidental platform diagnostic data. Network requests also expose an IP address to the receiving service.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and sync your journals, entries, moments, media, maps, and account.
  • Generate Smart Suggestions if you enable them.
  • Upload, store, display, and manage your photos and videos.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Provide support, respond to requests, and send important service notices.
  • Maintain, debug, protect, and improve the app.

Profiles, Sharing, and Collaboration

Stories start private. Your public profile name, public ID, and profile photo can be seen by other users. When you invite collaborators, they can access the shared draft and the editing history their role permits. Edits may be visible in that workspace before the owner reviews them. The owner controls each collaborator's approval setting. Publishing releases a version to the audience you select; later draft edits are separate from that published version.

Public stories can be accessed by anyone. Unlisted links can be used by anyone who has the link. Group membership, role, expiry, and blocking control other shared access. Recipients may take screenshots or export content; deleting or revoking content in Memory Bank cannot erase copies outside the service. Hidden audience content and reduced location precision apply to the published audience version, not to collaborators with access to the editable original.

We store invitation and membership records, comments, contributor attribution, editing history, audience choices, and the published snapshots needed to provide these features. Editing history is a recovery feature, not permanent archival storage: resolved history expires after 90 days and is subject to per-account storage and revision limits. Published story versions are retained until removed or affected by account deletion.

Content Safety and Reports

Before content is shared, shared text, captions, profile information, and photos may be sent to OpenAI's moderation service to detect harmful content. Private journal content and content hidden from the selected published audience are excluded from that check. Inviting an editor shares the editable original, so that shared original is checked. Images are accessed through short-lived links. Videos require manual review before sharing. OpenAI processes this data under its API data policies; see OpenAI's data controls.

We cache safety decisions and content fingerprints to avoid repeatedly sending unchanged content. Automated approvals expire after seven days. Rejected or pending decisions may be retained while the account exists or until an appeal is resolved. Reports include the reporting account, the reported person or content, your explanation, relevant content context, and the moderation decision. Authorized operators can investigate reports, remove content, or restrict sharing. Resolved report records expire after 90 days; account deletion removes account identifiers and submitted content from retained report records.

Report a story or person in the app, block a person from their profile, or contact bpmckee3@gmail.com. See the Community Guidelines. We do not sell personal information or use journal content, media, or location for third-party advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention and Deletion

Delete your account in You > Account > Delete Account, or use our external deletion request page without reinstalling the app. Recent sign-in may be required to confirm your identity. Account access is disabled when deletion is accepted. Cleanup then removes owned stories, stored versions, media, profile, relationships, invitations, comments, and associated records from active systems in resumable batches. We send a completion email when cleanup finishes.

Account-associated contributions are also removed or redacted from retained shared stories and stored copies within Memory Bank. Text attribution helps preserve other authors' words. Affected editing history and audience customizations may be cleared to prevent retained copies from exposing deleted material. Older content without attribution may require additional review. We cannot remove recipients' screenshots, exports, or independent copies stored outside Memory Bank.

For Sign in with Apple accounts, we request revocation of Apple's authorization. A token needed solely to complete revocation can be retained during provider outages and is deleted after revocation succeeds. Backup copies and service logs may persist for up to 90 days before routine rotation removes them. Records required by law or a specific security investigation may be retained only for that purpose. Contact us about a deletion that appears incomplete.

You may request a copy of your data in You > Account > Request a copy of my data or by emailing bpmckee3@gmail.com.

Your Choices

  • You can turn Smart Suggestions location use to Background, While Using, or Off in the app.
  • You can turn Photo Smart Suggestions On or Off in the app.
  • You can change location, photo, camera, microphone, and speech permissions in your device settings.
  • You can stop all app-based collection by uninstalling the app, subject to retained server-side account data until deleted.

Children

Memory Bank is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at bpmckee3@gmail.com so we can take appropriate action.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information processed by Memory Bank. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
