Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Memory Bank is a journaling app created by William McKee. This Privacy Policy explains what information Memory Bank collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at bpmckee3@gmail.com.

Information You Provide

Memory Bank stores the content you choose to create or upload, including journals, entries, moments, text fields, captions, locations, visits, photos, videos, and related metadata. This content is used to provide the journaling, timeline, map, media, and Smart Suggestions features in the app.

If you create an account or sign in, we may store account information such as your email address, user identifier, authentication provider information, and session information so you can access your account.

Location Information

Memory Bank can use location in several ways:

  • Manual location features: When you tag a moment, add a place, view nearby places, or use the map, Memory Bank may use your location while the app is open.
  • Smart Suggestions while using the app: If enabled, Memory Bank may use your location while the app is open to suggest moments based on places near you.
  • Background Smart Suggestions: If you choose the Background option, Memory Bank may use your location in the background, including when the app is closed, to detect places you visit and suggest moments to log.

You can control location suggestions in the app under You > Smart Suggestions. The available choices are Background, While Using, and Off. You can also change or revoke location permissions in your device settings.

Memory Bank does not sell your location data and does not use location data for advertising.

Photo Library, Camera, and Media

If you grant access, Memory Bank can use your photo library and camera so you can add photos or videos to moments and entries. If Photo Smart Suggestions are enabled, Memory Bank may scan your photo library while the app is open for photos or videos with location metadata and suggest moments you can log.

You can control photo suggestions in the app under You > Smart Suggestions. You can also change or revoke photo permissions in your device settings.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

If you use voice capture or transcription features, Memory Bank may request microphone and speech recognition permissions. Audio may be processed by the operating system or speech recognition provider to convert your voice into text. The resulting text may be saved in your Memory Bank content if you choose to keep it.

Place Search and Maps

Memory Bank uses map and place services to show maps, search for places, reverse geocode coordinates, and display place names. Depending on platform and configuration, this may involve Apple Maps, Google Maps, Radar, Geoapify, or similar place search services. Place queries and approximate coordinates may be sent to these services when needed to provide place search or place-name lookup. Memory Bank may cache provider responses to improve performance and reduce repeated provider calls.

Storage and Service Providers

Memory Bank uses service providers to run the app, authenticate users, store app data, store uploaded media, send email, and provide maps, place search, and speech-related functionality. These providers may process information only as needed to provide the service.

Current or expected service providers include Expo, Convex, Better Auth, Cloudflare R2 or Convex R2 storage, Apple sign-in, Google sign-in, Google Play Services, Apple Maps, Google Maps, Radar, Geoapify, and Resend.

Diagnostics and Automatically Collected Information

The app and its service providers may collect technical information needed to operate, secure, debug, and improve the service, such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, error logs, and basic usage events. If crash or error reporting is added, related diagnostic data may be collected to identify and fix issues.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and sync your journals, entries, moments, media, maps, and account.
  • Generate Smart Suggestions if you enable them.
  • Upload, store, display, and manage your photos and videos.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Provide support, respond to requests, and send important service notices.
  • Maintain, debug, protect, and improve the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, location information, journals, photos, videos, or other user content. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

We may share information with service providers as described above, when you direct us to do so, or when required by law or necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or others.

Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data and user content while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can delete individual content in the app where supported. You can delete your account from the app under You > Delete Account. Account deletion is intended to delete your account data and uploaded media from Memory Bank's systems.

You may also request a copy of your data from the app under You > Request a copy of my data or by contacting bpmckee3@gmail.com.

Your Choices

  • You can turn Smart Suggestions location use to Background, While Using, or Off in the app.
  • You can turn Photo Smart Suggestions On or Off in the app.
  • You can change location, photo, camera, microphone, and speech permissions in your device settings.
  • You can stop all app-based collection by uninstalling the app, subject to retained server-side account data until deleted.

Children

Memory Bank is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at bpmckee3@gmail.com so we can take appropriate action.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information processed by Memory Bank. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of Memory Bank after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Memory Bank's privacy practices, contact bpmckee3@gmail.com.
