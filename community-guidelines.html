Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Memory Bank helps people preserve and share memories. These rules apply to profiles, invitations, shared drafts, published stories, comments, and contributions.

Share with permission

Post only content you are entitled to use. Get appropriate permission to share another person's image or personal information. Respect the audience and access choices of others. You must be at least 13 to use the service.

Prohibited content and behavior

  • Child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation of minors, grooming, or attempts to facilitate child abuse. Never upload, forward, or attach suspected child abuse material to a support email.
  • Non-consensual sexual imagery, sexual exploitation, or sexually explicit content.
  • Credible threats, encouragement of violence or self-harm, graphic violence, or promotion of terrorism.
  • Hate targeting protected characteristics, harassment, bullying, stalking, or publishing private information without consent.
  • Impersonation, fraud, unlawful material, intellectual-property violations, spam, or attempts to evade moderation and access limits.

Report and block

Use Report content in a story's menu or Report person on a profile. A profile also offers Block. For public web stories, use the reporting link. You can report safety concerns without reinstalling the app by emailing bpmckee3@gmail.com. Include the public story or profile ID and a description; do not include passwords or private invitation tokens. For suspected child exploitation, describe where it appeared without sending the material itself. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.

How we respond

Shared text and photos undergo automated safety checks; videos need manual review before sharing. Human operators review reports and appeals. We prioritize imminent threats and child-safety reports. We may remove content, revoke sharing access, suspend an account, or take legally required reporting steps. Automated checks can make mistakes: contact the address above to appeal. Owner approval of an edit does not exempt it from these rules.

Privacy

Private journals and hidden audience content are excluded from shared-content screening. A draft deliberately shared with collaborators is shared content. See our Privacy Policy for providers, report data, retention, and deletion.
